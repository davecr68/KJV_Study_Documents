# 📖 KJV Bible Research Library
### Biblical Research • Scripture Studies • Historical Context • Supporting Documentation

**Welcome to the KJV Bible Research Library!**

This repository contains a growing collection of Bible research studies based primarily on the **King James Version (KJV)** of the Holy Bible.

The library currently includes **24 research studies**, with supporting documents intended to encourage deeper examination of Scripture, biblical language, historical context, and theological interpretation.

Our purpose is to provide accessible research materials for individuals who wish to investigate biblical subjects, compare Scripture with Scripture, and develop a more complete understanding of God's Word.

---

## 📚 About This Research Library

Each study investigates a particular biblical subject through careful examination of the available evidence.

Depending on the subject, research may include:

- **King James Version (KJV):** Examination of relevant biblical passages and their surrounding context.
- **Scripture Cross-References:** Comparisons of related passages throughout the Old and New Testaments.
- **Original Languages:** Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek word definitions, usage, and linguistic comparisons.
- **Strong's Concordance:** References to original-language terms and their appearances throughout Scripture.
- **Historical Context:** Cultural, geographical, and historical background relevant to the biblical text.
- **Ancient Manuscripts:** Examination of early textual witnesses when relevant.
- **Historical Writings:** References to ancient Jewish, Christian, and other historical literature when appropriate.
- **The Words of Jesus:** Special attention to the direct teachings and statements of Jesus Christ.
- **Theological Interpretations:** Examination of different perspectives while distinguishing interpretation from what Scripture explicitly states.

### Our Research Approach

We strive to distinguish between:

**1. What Scripture explicitly states.**

**2. What can reasonably be understood from its immediate and broader context.**

**3. What historical and linguistic evidence contributes to our understanding.**

**4. What represents theological interpretation, tradition, or speculation.**

The goal is not to impose a predetermined theological conclusion but to encourage careful study and thoughtful consideration of the biblical record.

## 📂 Finding the Research Documents

The repository contains the research documents associated with our KJV Bible study collection.

### Browse the Library

To locate a study:

1. Open the repository's main **Code** page.
2. Browse the available folders and files.
3. Select the document corresponding to your study topic.
4. Click the document to view its GitHub file page.
5. Select **Download raw file** or **Raw** to download the document, depending on the file format and GitHub interface.

You can also use GitHub's **Go to file** feature to search for a filename or study title.

### Download the Entire Research Library

To download all available studies:

1. Navigate to the repository's main page.
2. Select the green **Code** button.
3. Choose **Download ZIP**.
4. Extract the ZIP file to your computer.
5. Open the individual research documents using a compatible application.

This allows you to maintain a local copy of the research collection for personal reference and offline study.

## 🔎 How to Use These Studies

We encourage readers to approach each document with an open Bible and a willingness to examine the evidence.

### Recommended Study Method

**Step 1 — Read the Scripture**

Begin with the relevant passages in the King James Version.

**Step 2 — Examine the Context**

Consider the surrounding verses, chapter, intended audience, and historical setting.

**Step 3 — Compare Related Passages**

Allow Scripture to provide additional context through related teachings and accounts.

**Step 4 — Investigate Original-Language Terms**

Where applicable, compare Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek terms to understand their meanings and usage.

**Step 5 — Review Supporting Research**

Examine historical references, manuscript information, and scholarly interpretations.

**Step 6 — Evaluate the Conclusions**

Distinguish between directly supported biblical statements and interpretations drawn from the evidence.

> **"Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth."**
>
> — 2 Timothy 2:15 (KJV)

## 📖 Scripture as the Primary Reference

Although historical writings, linguistic resources, and scholarly research can provide valuable insight, these studies treat the canonical biblical text as the primary reference.

Other historical and religious writings may be examined to better understand ancient beliefs, terminology, historical circumstances, or the development of particular interpretations.

The inclusion of such material does not imply that it carries the same authority as canonical Scripture.

Whenever possible, the research distinguishes between:

- Canonical biblical passages
- Original-language evidence
- Historical documentation
- Later religious traditions
- Scholarly and theological interpretation

Readers are encouraged to independently examine the referenced materials.
